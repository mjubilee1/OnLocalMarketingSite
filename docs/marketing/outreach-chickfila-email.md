# Cold outreach — Chick-fil-A

## Before you send: three checks

1. **Title.** Dan Cathy stepped down as CEO in late 2021; Andrew Cathy is CEO and Dan
   moved to Chairman. Addressing him as "CEO" signals no research was done. Verify the
   current title on their newsroom/leadership page first.
2. **Address.** `dan.cathy@Chick-fil-A.com` looks pattern-guessed, not confirmed. Expect
   a bounce or an assistant filter. Corporate execs at this scale rarely read cold mail.
3. **Fit.** Our stated wedge — "compliance won't let us use cloud AI" — does not apply to
   a QSR. Do not use it here. The angles that *do* hold up:
   - Per-seat cloud-AI pricing is nonsensical for a 100+ person hourly team.
   - Many team members are minors; their records shouldn't go to a cloud vendor.
   - Training consistency across constant turnover is a genuine, expensive problem.

**Honesty guardrail:** we have no customers, testimonials, or results. Do not imply
otherwise, and do not cite made-up statistics about their turnover or operations.

## A. Email to Dan Cathy (as requested)

C-suite calibration: ultra-brief, understated, and the ask is a *referral* — never ask a
chairman to pilot software.

**Subject:** training consistency

Mr. Cathy,

Chick-fil-A's reputation depends on a team member's first weeks feeling the same in
every restaurant. That's a hard thing to hold at Operator scale, where a hundred-plus
person team turns over steadily and most training happens shoulder-to-shoulder with
whoever is on shift.

We built OnLocalAI for exactly that. It turns an Operator's own training material into a
searchable assistant, checklists, and quizzes — running on a single computer in the
restaurant. No cloud account, no per-seat AI fees for hourly staff, and no team member
information leaving the store.

We're in early access and would like a handful of Operators testing it.

Is there someone on the Operator-support side worth pointing this at?

— Montrell

customer, which is evidence this channel converts.

## Recommendation

Send **B**, not **A**. Ten Operators in one region is a far better use of the same hour
than one chairman, the ICP matches, and each one who says yes becomes the proof point we
currently don't have. If you want a corporate conversation later, an Operator already
using it is the credible way in.
