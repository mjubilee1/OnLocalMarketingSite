# Cold outreach — Libraries (early-access testers)

Target: public & academic libraries. Decision-makers: Library Director, Assistant
Director, Head of Public Services, Systems/IT Librarian.

**Why libraries are a real fit:** patron confidentiality is a professional duty (and
state law in most of the US), which rules out putting internal records or patron data
into cloud AI — while the service desk runs on part-timers, student workers, and
volunteers who each learned the policies from a different person.

**Honesty guardrail:** we have no customers or testimonials yet. Do not imply a client
base ("libraries I work with…"), invent results, or name-drop. The credible proof is the
one thing that's demonstrable: unplug the network and it still answers.

## What to personalize (in priority order)

1. **A live job posting** for part-time/desk/student staff — the strongest signal, and it
   connects straight to the onboarding problem. Check their careers page or Indeed.
2. **A published strategic plan or annual report** mentioning staff development,
   training, or technology.
3. **Their privacy/confidentiality policy page** — quote their own language back.
4. **Recent news**: a new branch, a systems migration, a new director.

If you can't find any of these, send the academic/technical variant instead — those lead
with a situation that's true of nearly every library, so they don't need a signal.

**Rule:** if you can delete the first line and the email still reads fine, the
personalization isn't doing any work. Rewrite it.
